`@voxolith/gen-grass` makes patches of ground cover. A patch is many independent blades, not one
connected object: tufts scatter over a disc, each tuft fans a handful of blades out of the ground,
and every blade is a single stem one voxel thick that bends under its own weight.

```ts
import { seededRandom } from "@voxolith/renderer/core";
import { generateGrass, PRESETS } from "@voxolith/gen-grass";

const { entity, stats } = generateGrass(PRESETS.meadow, seededRandom(42));
// entity.kind === "groundcover"; stats.blades, stats.flowers, stats.seedHeads
```

Unlike a tree, the result is deliberately many pieces. The guarantee is that every blade reaches the
ground, which is what the contract's grounding check asks.

## How it works

<Steps>
<Step>
**Tufts.** Tuft centres scatter over a disc of radius `footprint`, with a square-rooted radius so
the scatter is even rather than bunched at the centre. Each tuft fans its blades out of a small base
at `fanDeg` from vertical, at lengths between `lengthMin` and 1 of `height`.
</Step>
<Step>
**Blades.** Every blade is a level-0 stem grown by `growBranches` with no trunk rigidity: gravity
(`arc`) is the whole silhouette, from standing straight up to arching right over. A fern adds one
level of children in pairs down both sides of the midrib: its leaflets.
</Step>
<Step>
**Rasterise**, recording which blade wrote each voxel. Blades under a voxel radius are DDA lines,
6-connected by construction.
</Step>
<Step>
**Colour** along each blade, dark at the base to light at the tip, per blade rather than per patch.
Whole blades dry off, never individual voxels: speckling reads as noise rather than as a dry
season.
</Step>
<Step>
**Heads** on the tips of whole blades: flower heads in three colours (`flowers`) or elongated seed
heads (`seedHeads`). In winter, snow dusts the top faces.
</Step>
<Step>
**Prune** whatever does not reach the ground, flooding from the whole ground plane rather than from
one point, since the blades are independent.
</Step>
</Steps>

The look comes almost entirely from three things: how far blades splay from vertical (`fanDeg`), how
hard they arc over (`arc`), and the gradient along each blade. Voxel values are tuned for a 24-tall
patch and scale with `shape.height`.

## Presets

| preset | character |
|---|---|
| `grass` | a patch of lawn-like tufts |
| `meadow` | taller, more tufts, with flower heads and a few dry blades |
| `reeds` | tall, upright, barely fanned, with seed heads |
| `fern` | arching fronds carrying leaflets down both sides of a midrib |
| `dry` | short, heavily arched, mostly dried off, with small seed heads |

## Finer scales

At 50 and 100 voxels per metre the blades are redrawn from the skeleton as thin rods a voxel or two
across, rooted and arching like the coarse ones and shaded base to tip from them; flowers become
small petal discs and seed heads stay compact. Flowers, seed heads and snow are loose roles. See
[scales and refinement](/docs/generators/scales-and-refinement/).

## Development

```sh
bun run --cwd generators/grass verify           # every blade reaches the ground, budget, determinism
bun run --cwd generators/grass preview species  # contact sheets into previews/ (also: seeds, seasons)
bun run --cwd generators/grass gen              # bake .vox files into out/
```
