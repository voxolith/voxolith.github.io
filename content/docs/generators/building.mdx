`@voxolith/gen-building` makes houses. A small shape grammar lays out the mass (a plinth, hollow
storeys with floor slabs, a gable, hip or flat roof), then detail passes work over it at roughly 2.5
voxels to the foot, so a storey is about 24 voxels and a cottage sits in proportion beside a
190-voxel tree.

```ts
import { seededRandom } from "@voxolith/renderer/core";
import { generateBuilding, PRESETS } from "@voxolith/gen-building";

const { entity } = generateBuilding(PRESETS.farmhouse, seededRandom(42));
// rooms are hollow and closed; the front door faces +z
```

## How it works

<Steps>
<Step>
**Mass.** A plinth that stands a voxel proud of the walls, `storeys` hollow shells of
`storeyHeight` with floor slabs, and the roof shell as a stack of slabs each inset by the pitch
(`roofPitch` is run per unit rise, 1 being 45 degrees), with gables. The ridge runs along the longer
side.
</Step>
<Step>
**Layout.** Where every window and the door go is decided before any texture, on a rhythm of
`windowSpacing` with `windowFraction` of the slots actually cut, so a timber frame can frame them.
The door takes a ground-floor slot at the front; 14 voxels and wider makes a pair of boarded barn
doors.
</Step>
<Step>
**Walls.** Brick in running bond, ashlar courses of varying height, plaster that spalls to show brick
(`spalling`), or a timber frame with posts at every jamb and braced end bays. Mortar joints are
recessed and timbers stand proud (`relief`), so walls have relief rather than just colour. Walls are
addressed in face-local coordinates: along the face, up it, and depth into it from the outer skin.
</Step>
<Step>
**Trim.** Quoins at the corners, string courses at each floor line, a projecting plinth with a
water table, a corniced parapet with dentils under a flat roof.
</Step>
<Step>
**Openings.** Windows are set back in a reveal, framed, with glazing bars, a proud sill, a lintel or
soldier course, slatted shutters and flower boxes. The glass is real glass to the renderer and a
fraction of it (`lit`) glows. Doors get a panelled leaf with a glazed transom, a handle and a door
case, or boarded barn doors with Z braces; a hood on brackets, a lamp, and stone steps up the plinth.
</Step>
<Step>
**Roof.** Tile, slate or shingle laid in staggered courses with per-tile tone, or thatch with
streaks, ragged eaves and a ligger ridge. Ridge cap, barge boards, rafter tails, gutters and
downpipes; brick chimneys with a corbelled cap and hollow pots.
</Step>
<Step>
**Weather.** Streaks under sills, rising damp and tonal variation (`weathering`), moss on the roofs,
heavier low down and on the back slope, and on the plinth (`moss`).
</Step>
</Steps>

Everything random comes from the injected rng or from hashes of positions. `shape` and `openings`
decide which voxels exist and `look` mostly decides which role each gets. The four wall styles and
four roofings are separate from the geometry, and colours come from 32 roles (walls, mortar, trim,
beams, glass, lit glass, frames, doors, shutters, roof tones, ridge, chimney, soot, pots, foundation,
floor, moss, gutters, flowers and leaves), so a house restyles by palette. Four roles carry material
hints: glass is glass, lit glass emits, and the handle and rainwater goods are metal.

## Presets

| preset | walls and roof | shape |
|---|---|---|
| `cottage` | plaster, thatch | one storey, gable, one chimney, shutters and flower boxes |
| `farmhouse` | timber frame, tile | two storeys, two chimneys, gutters, a door hood |
| `townhouse` | brick with quoins, flat roof behind a corniced parapet | three storeys, tall narrow windows |
| `tower` | stone with quoins, slate | four storeys on a small square footprint, a steep hip roof |
| `barn` | timber frame, shingle | two tall storeys, few small windows, boarded double doors |

## Finer scales

At 20, 50 and 100 voxels per metre a building stays crisp (walls flat, corners sharp) and is refined on
its outside faces only; the rooms are skipped. What the finer grid adds is masonry and joinery at
their real size, which the coarse building can only suggest (its bricks are 80 cm long and its
joints 10 cm wide): bricks in running bond with one-voxel joints set back, ashlar courses of blocks
of varying length, fine plaster mottling, grain along timbers, tile, slate or shingle courses with a
shadowed lower edge, streaky ragged thatch, boards on doors and shutters and planks on floors. The
coarse building is generated without its relief first, since the joints are drawn at their own
scale. At 20 voxels per metre (5 cm) a course or a slate row at real size would be under two
voxels, so there the courses, tiles and boards have minimum sizes and come out at about twice
their real size ([at 20 voxels per metre](/docs/generators/scales-and-refinement/#at-20-voxels-per-metre)). Door panels and handles are tied to their frames after refinement, so
nothing on a door floats at any fine scale. Flowers and leaves are loose roles. See
[scales and refinement](/docs/generators/scales-and-refinement/).
