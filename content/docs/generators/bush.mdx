`@voxolith/gen-bush` grows shrubs. A bush is not a small tree: it has no trunk. Several stems leave
the ground together and lean outward, which is what gives a shrub its vase silhouette. A thicket
scatters more clumps of stems around the centre, at unequal sizes, so undergrowth reads as
undergrowth rather than as a ring of tidy bushes.

```ts
import { seededRandom } from "@voxolith/renderer/core";
import { generateBush, PRESETS } from "@voxolith/gen-bush";

const { entity, stats } = generateBush(PRESETS.thicket, seededRandom(42));
// entity.kind === "bush"; the anchor is the centre of the base, at ground level
```

Branch growth, clump placement, canopy carving and exposure shading come from
[gen-kit](/docs/generators/gen-kit/), shared with the tree and grass generators; what differs is the
base.

## How it works

<Steps>
<Step>
**Stems from the ground.** Each clump seeds a ring of level-0 stems within `baseSpreadRatio` of
its centre, each leaning out by `leanDeg` (plus a random spread) at a random azimuth. With
`clumps` above 0, satellite clumps are placed around the centre at 55% to 105% of the main
clump's size, which keeps a thicket from looking like identical bushes in a ring.
</Step>
<Step>
**Skeleton.** `growBranches` grows side shoots on each stem by the preset's branch levels, with a
little gravity and a little upward bend toward the tip, then `fitHeight` scales it to `height`
less the room the foliage needs on top.
</Step>
<Step>
**Wood.** Stems of at least a voxel radius are exact capsules; thinner ones are 6-connected DDA
lines, padded to two voxels when they are nearly one thick. Shrub stems are thin, so most of the
plant takes a flat per-stem twig colour; only the thicker basal wood gets a bark pattern. Thorns
are added outward from the surface, so they break the silhouette instead of speckling it.
</Step>
<Step>
**Foliage.** Leaf clumps are placed along the outermost branch level and, larger, at every tip.
Their density falls with `health`. The mass is hollowed below its hull, carved for sky holes and
shaded by exposure, with accent and dead leaves chosen per clump.
</Step>
<Step>
**Flowers, fruit and snow.** Blossom (in spring) and berries recolour the outer rim of whole
clumps, never single voxels, or they read as confetti. In winter, deciduous species drop their
leaves and take snow wherever a column of sky is clear above; the hedge stays green.
</Step>
<Step>
**Prune** everything not 6-connected to the ground, then crop with the anchor at the base centre.
</Step>
</Steps>

Parameters split into `shape` and `foliage` (which voxels exist) and `look` (which role each gets).
Voxel values are tuned for a 64-tall bush and scale with `shape.height`. Roles are stems (dark, mid,
light), twigs, thorns, leaves (light, mid, dark, edge, accent, dead), blossom, berries and snow;
seasons change the palette behind the roles, not the roles themselves.

## Presets

| preset | character |
|---|---|
| `bush` | a single rounded shrub |
| `thicket` | several staggered clumps of unequal size, for undergrowth |
| `bramble` | long arching canes, thorns and berries |
| `flowering` | blossom on whole clumps in spring |
| `hedge` | clipped: dense, low and wide, with almost no visible structure; evergreen, so it keeps its leaves in winter |

## Finer scales

At 20, 50 and 100 voxels per metre, leaves, blossom, berries and snow refine from the coarse voxels
(leaves as single leaves in the coarse shade, thorns as pairs of short needles); the stems are
redrawn from the skeleton as thin capsule shells, much slimmer than the coarse bush could draw them,
with faint streaks along them. Leaves, blossom, berries, snow and thorns are loose roles. See
[scales and refinement](/docs/generators/scales-and-refinement/).
