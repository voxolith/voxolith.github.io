---
title: The contract
description: Every check generators/contract runs against each registered entity generator, the quick, full and fine modes, and how to read a failure.
---

The engine and its hosts rely on a handful of promises from every `EntityGenerator`: stable
identity, honest roles, deterministic output, a model that stands on its base. The private
`generators/contract` package checks all of them for every registered generator in the repository.
It is not published; it exists so that a new generator, or a change to an old one, cannot quietly
break a host. The interface itself is documented with the engine under
[generator contract](/docs/engine/generator-contract/).

```sh
bun run --cwd generators/contract verify   # quick: defaults only (part of `bun run check`)
bun run --cwd generators/contract full     # every parameter extreme and every finer scale (CI)
bun run --cwd generators/contract fine     # defaults at the finer scales only
```

Each failure prints as `✗ what failed — detail`, and the run ends with a count and a list of every
problem. A generator is covered once its package is a dev dependency of the contract package and its
`register...Generators()` is called at the top of `tools/contract.ts`.

## What is checked

<Accordions type="multiple">
<Accordion title="Identity and roles">

- The id is namespaced: `voxolith/` followed by lower-case letters, digits, dots and dashes.
- The version is semver (`1.2.3`).
- It declares between 1 and 255 roles, with unique ids and colours in 0..1.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Defaults inside their own specs">

- Every `ParamSpec.path` exists in `defaults`, and the paths are unique.
- Every default equals `clampToSpec(spec, default)`: inside the range and on the step grid. A
  default off its grid would make a [share code](/docs/generators/share-codes/) of the default
  rebuild a different model. Fix the default, never the step.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Purity and determinism">

- The same seed gives the same voxels, even after another call with a different seed (no state
  leaks between calls).
- A different seed gives a different model.
- `generate` mutates neither the parameters it is passed nor the generator's `defaults`.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The model">

- `data` matches `size` and there are voxels at all.
- Every voxel value is a declared role, and the model's roles are the generator's roles in the
  declared order.
- The anchor lies inside the model's box.
- Nothing floats: at least 99.9% of voxels at the defaults (99% at a parameter extreme) are
  6-connected to the model's base, its two lowest layers. Several pieces that each stand on the
  ground, like the blades of a tuft or the stones of an outcrop, are fine.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Rigs and clips (rigged generators only)">

- 1..255 bones, ordered parents first, with unique ids; `model.bones` binds every voxel to a real
  bone.
- Every clip's tracks name real bones, their times ascend from 0, and their rotations are unit
  quaternions.
- A looping clip closes: its last key equals its first.
- A pose baked 40% into each clip stays in one piece (the largest part holds 98% of the voxels; a
  jump need not touch the ground).

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Share codes and the native context">

- `encodeState` then `decodeState` then `generateFromState` rebuilds exactly the model a direct
  call makes.
- A context of `{ voxelsPerMetre: 10 }` gives the byte-identical model a call without one does.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Every finer scale (full and fine)">

For each value in `scales`, at the defaults:
- the model is sparse (`sparse` set, `data` empty) and exactly k times the native model's size on
  every axis;
- every voxel value is a declared role, and the anchor is scaled with the model;
- it generates within 20 s and fits 96 MB of GPU bricks;
- at least 99% of the structure is grounded, with `looseRoles` (single leaves, petals, seed
  heads) neither counted nor carrying the flood;
- in `full`, a second run gives the same bricks.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Every parameter extreme (full only)">

Each spec is set, one at a time, to its `min` and `max` (both values of a `bool`, every option of an
`enum`) on top of the defaults. Each must still generate without throwing and pass the model checks
above, with the 99% grounding threshold.

</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Not covered

`@voxolith/gen-terrain` is not an entity generator: it describes a region (a heightfield and
water), takes a seed rather than an rng, and has its own checks in `bun run --cwd generators/terrain
verify` (shape, river continuity, `fillBrick` against `roleAt`, determinism, picking).

The contract says nothing about whether a model looks right. Each package's own `verify` adds
generator-specific checks (budgets, knobs that must respond, a building's hollowness and openings, a
creature's hidden interior and footfalls), and the [preview renderer](/docs/generators/gen-kit/preview/)
is how a person judges the look.
